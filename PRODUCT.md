# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The personal site of Yaroslav Yermilov, written for four overlapping readers:

- **Tech peers & community** — fellow engineers and speakers who arrive for the
  writing (EN + UA), the interactive labs, and the games, and stay for the craft.
- **Clients & employers** — people evaluating Yaroslav's work; the site is the
  credibility layer behind hiring, consulting, and collaboration.
- **Workshop & talk leads** — conference organizers and workshop buyers who find
  him through his talks and follow the workshop CTA.
- **Yaroslav himself** — this is a 5+ year *field journal*: a durable, self-owned
  archive of writing, talks, books, and experiments. Longevity and ownership
  outrank conversion.

Their context is unhurried and intentional: someone reading a post end to end,
poking at a lab, or browsing the timeline of talks — not skimming a funnel.

## Product Purpose

A long-lived personal home on the web — blog, lab, games, talks, books, photos,
and a workshop prospectus — organized as a **Field Journal** of shared-timeline
"volumes" (writing-talking, inspiration, personal, workshop). It exists so
Yaroslav's thinking and making have a permanent, self-owned address that reads as
carefully as it's built. Success = a visitor trusts the person behind it, reads
more than they planned to, and the archive still feels right five years from now.

## Brand Personality

**Craftsman's field journal — tactile, editorial, precise, unhurried.**

The voice is a working maker writing in the open: confident without swagger,
specific over generic, warm but exacting. Typography does the talking (Newsreader
serif for reading, JetBrains Mono for the machinery); the paper-and-ink surface,
timeline spine, and mono date stamps evoke a bound notebook more than a product
page. It should feel handmade and considered — the design *is* the argument that
the person sweats details.

## Anti-references

This should NOT look like:

- **A generic dev-portfolio template** — dark hero, neon gradient, "I build cool
  things," a card grid of repos.
- **A corporate SaaS landing** — all-caps eyebrow kickers stacked above every
  section, feature grids, stock illustration.
- **The loud AI-generated aesthetic** — purple/glassmorphism gradients, emoji
  headers, the saturated 2024 default look.

Restraint over spectacle: when in doubt, quieter and more editorial.

## Design Principles

1. **The craft is the pitch.** Every detail is evidence the maker is meticulous;
   sloppiness anywhere undercuts the whole argument.
2. **Reading is the primary act.** Type, measure, and rhythm serve sustained
   reading first; interactivity earns its place, it doesn't crowd the page.
3. **One coherent identity across surfaces.** Blog, labs, and games share the same
   tokens ("Tactile Workbench") so the site reads as one bound volume, even where
   labs turn louder.
4. **Built to outlast trends.** Choices favor a 5-year horizon and the
   lowest-magic path over whatever is fashionable this year.
5. **Bilingual and equal.** EN and UA are first-class; nothing assumes English is
   the "real" version.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** for contrast, focus, and semantics.
- Honors `prefers-color-scheme` — automatic light/dark, both hand-tuned (a louder
  dark palette on the lab surface).
- Honors `prefers-reduced-motion` — animation is opt-out-aware, never essential.
- Always-visible focus rings (`--focus-ring` token); keyboard-navigable.
- Bilingual EN/UA with correct `lang`, `hreflang`, and `og:locale` (the `uk-UA`
  BCP-47 tag drives markup even though the UI label is "UA").
