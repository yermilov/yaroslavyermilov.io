
                        ABOUT THE GAME

   BACCARAT — a card game. Two players, each is dealt
   three cards from a 54-card deck (two jokers).

   Card points:
        2-10  — face value
        Jack — 11,  Queen — 12,  King — 13,  Ace — 14
        Joker — 25

   You bet on the outcome: player 1 wins (f),
   player 2 wins (s) or a draw (n).

   Right guess — the bet is added to your balance, else subtracted.

   Starting balance — 500 hryvnias.
