Two weeks since I've joined Preply as Principal Engineer.

From the first meetings with the Preplers I was amazed by the ambition in product vision and human-centred mission to connect learners with tutors. This is combined with incredible engineering culture and boldness to figure out hardest engineering problems and step out of old patterns. Of course, this all goes together with being a Ukrainian-originated company 💜

My first big mission is to help with Agentic Engineering transformation of our SDLC process and I couldn't be more excited to jump into it!
